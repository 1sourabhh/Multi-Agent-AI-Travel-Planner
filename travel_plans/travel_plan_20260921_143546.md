## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival & First Glimpse of Paris**  
**Morning**  
1. **Arrive at Charles‑de‑Gaulle (CDG) or Orly (ORY).**  
2. Take the **RER B** train (≈ 30 min) to **Saint‑Michel‑Notre‑Dame** – buy a single‑ticket “Paris Visite” zone 1‑2 (≈ €1.90) or a Navigo Découverte pass (if staying >5 days).  
3. Check‑in at **Hotel Caron de Beaumarchais** (store luggage if early arrival).  

**Mid‑Morning** – *Île de la Cité*  
* Walk to **Notre‑Dame** (exterior; interior currently closed for restoration).  
* Pop into **Sainte‑Chapelle** (Gothic stained‑glass masterpiece – €11).  

**Lunch** – *Latin Quarter*  
* **Le Petit Cler** (classic bistro, try the Croque‑Monsieur).  

**Afternoon** – *Historic Core*  
* Wander the **Bouquinistes** stalls along the Seine.  
* Visit the **Conciergerie** (former prison, €9).  

**Evening** – *Seine River Stroll*  
* Walk across **Pont Neuf** to **Pont des Arts** for sunset views.  
* Dinner at **Le Coupe Chou** (cozy, traditional French).  

**Optional Nightcap** – Grab a glass of wine at **Le Bar à Vin** near the hotel.  

---

### **Day 2 – The Grand Classics**  
**Morning** – *The Louvre & Gardens*  
* Arrive early (opens 09:00) – pre‑book a timed‑entry ticket (€17).  
* Focus on highlights: **Mona Lisa**, **Venus de Milo**, **Winged Victory**.  
* Exit onto the **Tuileries Garden** – rent a chair, sip coffee at **Café Richelieu**.  

**Lunch** – *Palais Royal*  
* **Le Grand Véfour** (Michelin-starred, pricey) or **Café Kitsuné** for a lighter bite.  

**Afternoon** – *Avenue des Champs‑Élysées*  
* Metro Line 1 to **Charles de Gaulle – Étoile**.  
* Walk up the **Champs‑Élysées** to the **Arc de Triomphe** (climb for €13).  

**Evening** – *Rooftop Views*  
* Take Metro Line 1 to **Rennes** → walk to **Le Perchoir Marais** (rooftop bar, €12‑15 cocktails).  
* Light dinner of **tapas** at **Le Baratin** (near the bar).  

---

### **Day 3 – Art, Gardens & Bohemian Vibes**  
**Morning** – *Musée d’Orsay*  
* Take RER C to **Musée d’Orsay** (opens 09:30, €16).  
* Marvel at Impressionist masterpieces: Monet, Renoir, Van Gogh.  

**Mid‑Morning** – *Rodin Museum*  
* Walk (15 min) to **Musée Rodin** – see *The Thinker* (ticket €13).  

**Lunch** – *Saint‑Germain‑des‑Prés*  
* **Café de Flore** – iconic literary café; try the **salade niçoise**.  

**Afternoon** – *Explore Saint‑Germain*  
* Browse boutique shops, **Les Deux Magots**, **Le Bon Marché** (upscale department store).  
* Optional: **Luxembourg Garden** (short metro ride to **Odéon**).  

**Evening** – *Jazz Night*  
* Dinner at **Le Relais de l'Entrecôte** (steak‑frites).  
* Head to **Le Caveau de la Huchette** (historic jazz cellar, entry €15).  

---

### **Day 4 – Montmartre & Modern Paris**  
**Morning** – *Montmartre & Sacré‑Coeur*  
* Metro Line 12 to **Abbesses** → walk up the hill (or take the funicular).  
* Visit **Basilique du Sacré‑Coeur** (free) – panoramic city view.  

**Mid‑Morning** – *Place du Tertre*  
* Watch artists at work, maybe get a quick portrait (€50‑€120).  

**Lunch** – *Le Consulat* (historic café on Place du Tertre).  

**Afternoon** – *Canal Saint‑Martin & Le Marais*  
* Metro Line 4 to **Strasbourg‑Saint‑Denis**, then walk along the **Canal Saint‑Martin** – trendy cafés, street art.  
* Return to **Le Marais** for boutique shopping (Merci, The Broken Arm).  

**Evening** – *Seine Dinner Cruise*  
* Book a **Bateaux‑Mouches** dinner cruise (≈ €70, includes 3‑course meal, live music).  
* After the cruise, enjoy a nightcap at **Bar Hemingway** (Ritz) if you’re feeling extra luxe.  

---

### **Day 5 – Last‑Minute Shopping & Departure**  
**Morning** – *Galeries Lafayette & Printemps*  
* Metro Line 9 to **Chaussée d'Antin – La Fayette**.  
* Browse the famous **Galeries Lafayette** dome, grab a quick pastry at **Ladurée** (macarons!).  

**Late Morning** – *Le Marais Souvenirs*  
* Return to hotel, pack, check‑out (store luggage at the front desk if your flight is later).  
* Stroll through **Place des Vosges**, pick up a few artisanal goodies (soap, cheese).  

**Lunch** – *Quick Bite*  
* **L’As du Fallafel** (legendary falafel sandwich, 5‑minute line).  

**Afternoon** – *Transfer to Airport*  
* Retrieve luggage, take **RER B** from **Saint‑Michel‑Notre‑Dame** to **CDG** (≈ 35 min).  
* Arrive at least **2 hours** before your international departure.

### Practical Info  

* **Language:** French; most staff speak English in tourist zones.  
* **Currency:** Euro (€). ATMs are plentiful; use a no‑foreign‑transaction‑fee card.  
* **Power plugs:** Type C/E, 230 V, 50 Hz. Bring a universal adapter.  
* **Safety:** Pickpocketing is common in crowded areas (Metro, tourist sites). Keep wallets in front pockets or a money belt.  
* **Health:** EU travel insurance recommended; pharmacies (“Pharmacie”) are open late.
